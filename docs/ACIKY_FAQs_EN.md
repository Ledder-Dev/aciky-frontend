# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — ACIKY
### Cuban Alliance of Kundalini Yoga Instructors (aciky.org)

---

## 1. About ACIKY

**What is ACIKY?**
ACIKY is the Cuban Alliance of Kundalini Yoga Instructors, an organization that brings together KRI-certified instructors in Cuba to organize, strengthen, and expand the practice of Kundalini Yoga throughout the island.

**Does ACIKY have another official name?**
Yes. ACIKY is also the National Kundalini Yoga Teachers Association of Cuba (NKYTA), affiliated with IKYTA — the International Kundalini Yoga Teachers Association.

**When and how was ACIKY founded?**
It was officially established on June 26, 2025. It grew out of the need to unify Cuban instructors who, for years, had independently organized gatherings, retreats, classes, and workshops. In 2024, encouragement from Gurusansar Singh and an invitation from 3HO to an international gathering led the Cuban community to formally constitute itself as an Alliance (given the legal complexity of forming associations in Cuba) under a unified vision of the Kundalini Yoga legacy and lineage. The Faro de Luz Festival, already held before then, was recognized as the new organization's flagship event.

**What is Kundalini Yoga?**
It's an ancient practice that combines physical postures (kriyas), breathing techniques (pranayama), meditation, and mantras to awaken vital energy and elevate consciousness, reducing stress and promoting greater balance and purpose.

**Who taught this lineage of Kundalini Yoga?**
ACIKY teaches Kundalini Yoga as transmitted by Master Yogi Bhajan (1929–2004), who brought this tradition from East to West in 1969.

**What is ACIKY's mission?**
To elevate and empower Kundalini Yoga teachers in Cuba, supporting their practice, strengthening yoga communities, and sharing the sacred science of Kundalini Yoga in every corner of the country.

**Does ACIKY have a single leader?**
No. Decisions are made collectively through a Steering Committee (President, Vice President, and committee members), without a single leadership figure.

**Does ACIKY follow guiding principles?**
Yes, eleven principles that include: creating a supportive environment for every instructor to reach their full potential, fostering cooperation and self-guidance, involving those affected by a decision, avoiding advantages or disadvantages for any individual or group, keeping information flow transparent, and resolving conflicts through deep listening and respectful communication.

**How many members and instructors does ACIKY have?**
The community includes active members and instructors trained since 2016; exact figures are updated in the "About Us" section of the site.

**Which organizations does ACIKY collaborate with?**
Its partnerships include Casa de Asia (ACIKY's official headquarters since 2019), the Office of the Historian of Havana, the Embassy of India in Cuba, IKYTA, 3HO, the Cuban Yoga Association, Om Meditación, Basanti Escuela, Dluzverde, Centro Árbol, Somos Imperfectos, and community health and special education projects that bring Kundalini Yoga to children and young people.

**In which Cuban provinces does ACIKY have a presence?**
ACIKY currently has a presence in 3 provinces (Havana, Matanzas, and Cienfuegos) and is actively working to expand to all 15 provinces of the country through the Golden Routes project.

## 2. In-person classes and practice

**How can I see the class schedule?**
The "Classes" section of the site (aciky.org/pages/schedule.html) publishes the updated weekly schedule.

**Where are classes held?**
In-person classes and meditations take place at venues such as the Casa Museo de Asia, among other practice spaces ACIKY continues to add across Cuba (see the "Other Spaces" section).

**Is the first class free?**
Yes, ACIKY offers a free first class so newcomers can try the practice with no commitment.

**What should I bring to class?**
It's recommended to arrive 10 minutes early, wear comfortable clothing, bring a blanket, and avoid eating for 2 hours before practice.

**Do I need to reserve my spot?**
Yes, it's recommended to confirm your reservation via WhatsApp before attending (each class has a "Reserve" button that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message).

**Are classes suitable for beginners?**
Yes, classes are designed for all levels: each class indicates whether it's Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or All Levels.

**Do classes have a cost?**
Each class shows its price (or "Free" if applicable) and, when applicable, a special price for ACIKY members.

## 3. Online Sadhanas (daily virtual practice)

**What is Sadhana?**
It's a daily spiritual practice done during the Ambrosial Hours — or Amrit Vela, "the nectar time" — the periods from 4:00 to 7:00 AM and 4:00 to 7:00 PM, when energy is calmest and most receptive. ACIKY offers it virtually to connect the community regardless of location.

**What time is ACIKY's sadhana?**
7:30 to 9:00 AM (Havana time), because the session is guided by a certified teacher joining from Mexico.

**What platform are sadhanas held on?**
They're held over Zoom.

**How do I join the Online Sadhanas?**
Contact ACIKY (via WhatsApp) to receive the access link for the daily sessions.

**What do I need to participate?**
A device with a stable internet connection, clear audio (headphones or speakers), and keeping your microphone muted during the session to preserve the collective atmosphere.

**What's the structure of a sadhana?**
It follows the traditional sequence: a selected pauree (recited 11 times), opening, healing and wellness prayer, warm-up, kriya, meditation, and closing with prayer and reflection.

**Is there a recommended minimum commitment?**
Yes, it's suggested to commit for 40 consecutive days at first to build the habit and experience the full benefits of the practice.

**Can I start gradually?**
Yes, newcomers are encouraged to join Sunday sessions first and then progressively add more days.

**What preparation is recommended before a sadhana?**
Lay out your clothes and prepare your space the night before, place your alarm clock away from your bed, drink a glass of water upon waking, and wait about 15 minutes before practicing.

**What benefits does regular sadhana practice bring?**
Greater mental clarity, elevated energy, spiritual connection, inner discipline, emotional stability, sacred community, clear purpose, and a greater capacity for manifestation.

## 4. Golden Routes (national expansion)

**What are the Golden Routes?**
It's ACIKY's expansion project that brings Kundalini Yoga to communities across every Cuban province, through certified instructors who travel to teach and help establish local practice groups.

**How does the process work?**
It consists of six stages: identifying the need, planning the route, travel and immersion, intensive teaching, establishing a local group, and ongoing follow-up and support.

**How can I get involved in the Golden Routes?**
There are four ways to participate:
- **Traveling instructor:** requires a current KRI certification, at least 1 year of experience, and availability to travel.
- **Local host:** offering space for classes, coordinating local participants, or helping with lodging.
- **Emerging leader:** receiving free specialized training, materials, and ongoing support to sustain the practice in your community.
- **Sponsor:** financially supporting transportation, educational materials, or equipment for practice spaces.

**What's the goal of the Golden Routes?**
The vision unfolds progressively: continue covering the 15 Cuban provinces (2026 Goal), complete coverage of all 15 provinces (2027 Goal), and build a self-sustaining network where every province has its own instructors (2028 Goal).

## 5. Rebirthing Ceremonies

**What is a Rebirthing Ceremony?**
It's a transformative experience symbolizing the "death" of the limited ego and the birth of an expanded consciousness, using breathwork (pranayama), mantras, meditation, and a symbolic release process ("transformative fire").

**How does the rebirthing ceremony process work?**
It follows five steps: sacred preparation (meditation and intention-setting), transformative breathing (pranayama), mantras of liberation, the "transformative fire" (symbolically releasing fears and limiting patterns), and final rebirth and integration.

**What benefits does this experience offer?**
Emotional release of traumas and blockages, deep transformation of the self, expanded spiritual connection, and healing of ancestral or family patterns.

**Who is this experience intended for?**
People going through life transitions, those seeking to heal emotional or family patterns, those seeking clearer purpose, and, in general, any spiritual seeker committed to their growth.

**How do I find out about upcoming dates?**
Upcoming ceremonies are announced in the corresponding section of the site; availability can also be checked directly via WhatsApp.

## 6. Faro de Luz Festival

**What is the Faro de Luz Festival?**
It's ACIKY's most important annual yoga and wellness event: 3 days of full immersion combining ancestral teachings, intensive practices, sacred music, and the collective energy of the community, with participants from all over Cuba and other countries.

**When is it held?**
Traditionally in October, though the exact dates, venue, and theme for each edition are confirmed and published on the festival page, since this information is updated every year.

**What does festival participation include?**
All Kundalini Yoga sessions, specialized workshops, sacred music sessions, group meditations, organic vegetarian meals, shared-room accommodation, and access to the private post-festival community.

**Is there any benefit to registering early?**
Check the festival page to see whether the current edition offers early-registration benefits, since these vary from year to year.

**Can I support the festival without attending?**
Yes, through a donation via the site's donations section.

## 7. Membership for instructors

**Who can become an ACIKY member?**
ACIKY accepts as members Kundalini Yoga instructors certified by the KRI (Kundalini Research Institute) at level 1 or higher — the only essential requirement.

**How do I join as a certified instructor?**
Message ACIKY via WhatsApp or email to start the membership process. You can also check the membership guide available on the site (aciky.org/pages/membership.html).

**What does participation as a member involve?**
Participation is not mandatory, but it's highly valued. Members contribute in various ways: organizing activities, leading classes, serving the community, attending events, promoting the practice, and exchanging ideas and suggestions within the collective.

**Is anything asked of members in return?**
For now, the only thing ACIKY asks of its members is to offer a special price for fellow ACIKY members in their own classes and events. As the organization grows, fair compensation arrangements for shared work will be explored.

**Does ACIKY charge a membership fee?**
Contributions to ACIKY (including those arising from events promoted among members) are voluntary — each person contributes according to their own conscience and desire to sustain the mission.

**What if I don't have KRI certification yet but want to teach someday?**
ACIKY guides those who feel called to become instructors on the path to KRI certification; it's recommended to contact us directly for more information.

**Does ACIKY have an evaluation or professional development team?**
Yes, it has a CEU Evaluation Team focused on training and teaching, service (seva) and leadership, research, and additional benefits for the most active instructors. This team also reviews and validates events, workshops, and blog articles that members want ACIKY to promote or publish.

**Who leads ACIKY?**
A President and Vice President, along with the Steering Committee, open to any member who feels called to contribute.

## 8. Donations and financial support

**How can I financially support ACIKY?**
Through the site's donations section (aciky.org/pages/donations.html), via PayPal or bank transfer/Transfermóvil in CUP.

**Why donate to a personal PayPal account instead of an ACIKY account?**
Due to the financial restrictions ACIKY faces as a Cuban organization, it currently cannot have its own PayPal account. A teacher who believes in the mission offers the use of their personal account to receive international donations on ACIKY's behalf (the note must state exactly "Donación ACIKY"). Every cent donated goes entirely to the community.

**What is my donation used for?**
Instructor training and development, the Faro de Luz Festival, the Golden Routes, regular classes and programs, the Langar (sacred meal for participants), and special ceremonies and events.

**What do I receive if I donate?**
Optional public recognition on the site and social media, a donation certificate, updates on funded projects, and a special invitation to ACIKY events and ceremonies.

**How long does it take to confirm my donation?**
ACIKY verifies payments manually against its PayPal history; confirmation can take up to 48 hours.

**Are donations tax-deductible or refundable?**
No. They go to ACIKY's general fund to finance activities, events, and community upkeep; they are not tax-deductible nor refundable once confirmed.

## 10. Blog, testimonials, postures, and videos

**Who can publish on ACIKY's blog?**
Only instructors and administrators authorized by ACIKY. Every article must align with Kundalini Yoga teachings as taught by Yogi Bhajan, and the ACIKY CEU Tribunal reviews and validates it before publication.

**Can I leave my testimonial on the site?**
Yes, once logged in you can write your experience from the Testimonials page. Each testimonial is reviewed before publication.

**Where can I find postures and tutorial videos?**
In the "Postures" and "Videos" sections of the site, both with a search feature by name.

## 11. Account, privacy, and data

**What data does ACIKY collect when I register?**
First name, last name, and spiritual name, email address (for authentication and communications), an optional profile photo (stored on Cloudinary), any testimonials you choose to publish, and a functional session cookie to keep you logged in.

**Does ACIKY use tracking or advertising cookies?**
No. Only a functional session cookie is used, necessary for logging in — no tracking or third-party advertising cookies.

**Can I delete my account and my data?**
Yes, at any time, by contacting ACIKY via WhatsApp or the contact page.

**What's the minimum age required to create an account?**
18 years old.

**I forgot my password, what do I do?**
Use the "Forgot your password?" link on the login screen to receive a password reset email.

**Do I need to verify my email address?**
Yes, after registering you'll receive a verification email; if it doesn't arrive, you can resend it from the login screen.

**Do I need to create an account to participate?**
The site allows logging in or registering for access to a personal profile, though booking your free first class or joining sadhanas only requires messaging via WhatsApp.
